Tractor Cleanliness Checklist

CLEANLINESS EXPECTATIONS
You are not permitted to use tobacco or e-cigarette products in a tobacco free tractor.
To be completed when starting/finishing or when switching tractors and turned in to manager. Tractor should be clean and ready for the next driver assigned to this unit. If the tractor does not meet the expectations below, please notify a manager.

Action Items

1) All surface areas wiped down to include dash, steering wheel, shift lever, etc.
2) Inside windows are clean and wiped down
3) Floors and seats vacuumed
4) Jump seat clear of items and clutter
5) All bedding removed
6) All personal clothing removed (closet should be empty)
7) All food items removed (perishable and non-perishable)
8) Refrigerator is empty
9) Storage compartments are empty
10) Bottles and cups are removed
11) Cleaning kit stocked with items as needed using Comdata card
12) As applicable, stow snow chains in working order/properly replaced
13) Odor free
14) Wash tractor
15) Fuel tractor
16) Return tractor to designated parking spot
